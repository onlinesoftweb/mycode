## ❓ Common Questions

### "I've never coded before. Is this too hard?"
**No!** We designed this specifically for beginners. Start with the README, then follow the learning path above.

### "Which programming language should I learn first?"
Start with **HTML and CSS** (they're not even "programming" - they're markup/styling). Then move to **JavaScript**. PHP and WordPress come later.

### "How long will it take to learn?"
Everyone learns at their own pace. With 30 minutes per day, you can understand the basics in 1-2 months and start making your own modifications in 2-3 months.

### "What if I get stuck?"
1. Read the documentation again carefully
2. Check the "Troubleshooting" section in GETTING_STARTED.md
3. Look at QUICK_REFERENCE.md for tips
4. Google your error message
5. Ask on Stack Overflow or Reddit r/learnprogramming

### "Can I break anything?"
Not really! You can always re-download the files or revert changes. Breaking things and fixing them is actually a great way to learn!

## 💡 Learning Tips

1. **Go Slow**: Don't rush. Understanding is more important than speed.
2. **Code Daily**: Even 15 minutes a day is better than 2 hours once a week.
3. **Type, Don't Copy**: Actually type out the examples - you'll learn more.
4. **Experiment**: Change things and see what happens!
5. **Read Errors**: Error messages tell you exactly what's wrong.
6. **Take Breaks**: If you're frustrated, step away and come back later.
7. **Celebrate Wins**: Every small success is progress! 🎉
